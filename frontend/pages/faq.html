Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Topic: Architecture, Open Source & Operations Author: Samrudh Dwivedula
What is OmniRevive-OS?
OmniRevive-OS is an autonomous AI-powered revenue recovery control plane designed to detect, diagnose, and recover failed payment transactions across consumer checkout and enterprise B2B accounts receivable.
How does OmniRevive-OS recover failed payments?
OmniRevive-OS operates a dual-speed architecture: a Fast-Loop (<50ms) that uses Qdrant vector memory and SciPy Weibull survival models to reroute or retry transient checkout failures, and a Deep-Loop that deploys trilingual voice AI agents in Telugu, Hindi, and English to resolve disputed B2B invoices.
How does OmniRevive-OS prevent duplicate charging or double debits?
OmniRevive-OS implements an atomic Compare-And-Swap (CAS) distributed mutex in Redis alongside HMAC signature validation and SHA-256 idempotency tokens. This guarantees that parallel webhook events can never trigger duplicate recovery attempts.
What is the AWS Cedar Policy Gatekeeper?
The AWS Cedar Policy Gatekeeper is a deterministic authorization engine that strictly enforces business boundaries (maximum discount caps, TRAI quiet hours between 21:00 and 09:00 IST, and CFO dual-key quorum signoffs) independent of the AI models.
What payment rails and gateways are supported?
OmniRevive-OS provides normalized adapters for Razorpay, Juspay HyperSDK, PhonePe Switch, Cashfree Payments, CRED, and Stripe, with simulated NPCI UPI switch telemetry and live webhook routing.
Is OmniRevive-OS open source?
Yes, OmniRevive-OS is an open-source project released under the MIT license and developed by Samrudh Dwivedula for the Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026.
