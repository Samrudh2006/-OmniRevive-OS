About OmniRevive-OS

Author: Samrudh Dwivedula Competition: Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026 (Track 03) License: MIT Open Source

OmniRevive-OS was engineered from the ground up to solve one of the largest silent revenue leaks in Indian and global fintech: uncoordinated, naive payment retry loops that alienate customers, trigger punitive bank penalty charges, and leave ₹18,000+ Crores on the table every year.

The Engineering Philosophy

We believe autonomous systems handling financial flows must be bound by deterministic mathematics, not unbounded LLM generation. By decoupling language understanding from execution invariants (via AWS Cedar, Redis CAS mutexes, and SHA-256 Merkle audit trees), OmniRevive-OS proves that autonomous fintech software can achieve both cutting-edge recovery rates and institutional-grade safety.
