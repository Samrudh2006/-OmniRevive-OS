B2B Payment Dispute Resolution & Neural Voice AI FSM

Topic: Enterprise Accounts Receivable & Voice AI Author: Samrudh Dwivedula Published: September 2026

In B2B business workflows, failed invoices cannot simply be retried against a credit card. B2B payments frequently stall due to GSTIN tax filing mismatches, purchase order (PO) discrepancies, milestone verification delays, or cashflow timing. These disputes require intelligent multi-turn negotiation governed by formal financial bounds.

1. The Deterministic B2B State Machine (FSM)

To avoid hallucinated commitments, OmniRevive-OS decouples LLM language processing from financial state execution. The negotiation flow is governed by a formal SQLite WAL Finite State Machine:

2. Trilingual Autonomous Voice Agent

Indian commerce operates across multilingual ecosystems. OmniRevive-OS features an autonomous voice negotiation studio capable of fluent, natural dialogues in:

  • Telugu (te-IN): Natural inflection for Hyderabad and Andhra Pradesh merchant corridors.
  • Hindi (hi-IN): Conversational enterprise negotiation for North and Central Indian trade hubs.
  • Indian English (en-IN): Formal, accent-tuned enterprise communication.

3. 3-Tier Financial Policy Bounds

Every concession negotiated by the voice agent is pre-validated by the AWS Cedar policy gatekeeper:

  • Max Concession: Maximum allowable early settlement discount is capped at 5.0% (configurable per enterprise merchant).
  • Executive Dual-Key Signoff: Any invoice restructuring exceeding ₹50,000 requires dual-key cryptographic approval from the enterprise CFO.
  • Non-Coercive Calling Policy: Maximum 2 call attempts per 48 hours; zero contact permitted between 21:00 and 09:00 IST.

4. Live Settlement & Promise-to-Pay (PTP) Tracking

When a customer locks in a Promise-to-Pay date, OmniRevive-OS generates a calendar sync payload, issues an instant WhatsApp payment confirmation link with dynamic UPI QR, and sets a high-priority retry timer synchronized with the customer's agreed settlement date.
