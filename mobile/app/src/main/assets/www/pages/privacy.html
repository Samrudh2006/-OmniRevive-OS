Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 23, 2026 · Version 1.2.0-Production

1. Executive Overview

OmniRevive-OS is an autonomous revenue recovery control plane engineered under strict zero-trust principles. We adhere to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023, RBI Data Localization Directives, and GDPR standards.

2. Zero-PII Vector Memory Guarantee

All transaction events ingested via webhook are cryptographically masked before semantic similarity evaluation in Qdrant Vector DB. Customer Phone Numbers, Virtual Payment Addresses (VPAs), PAN, and Credit Card Primary Account Numbers (PANs) are salted with SHA-256 HMAC tokens and never stored in plaintext vector memory.

3. Data Localization & Sovereign Infrastructure

All payment diagnostic data, state mutexes, and voice FSM audio buffers are processed within sovereign Indian data centers: AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) and AWS Hyderabad (ap-south-2). No payment data is transmitted outside Indian jurisdiction.

4. Voice AI Data Retention

Deep-Loop voice agent interactions (Telugu, Hindi, and English) are synthesized ephemerally. Audio recordings are held in volatile in-memory buffers during the negotiation turn and permanently destroyed once the Promise-to-Pay (PTP) hash is recorded in the Merkle audit ledger.

5. Cryptographic Ledger Integrity

Transaction state transformations are sealed into a tamper-evident SHA-256 Merkle chain. This ledger contains only mathematical proofs, event timestamps, and policy decisions, providing verifiable compliance without exposing raw financial credentials.
